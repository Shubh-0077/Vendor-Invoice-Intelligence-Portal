Invoice Risk Assessment

Classify a vendor invoice as safe for automatic approval or as a candidate for manual review, based on cost, freight, and delivery-timing signals.

Business insight

Freight cost scales predictably with order size, but not evenly per unit. Bottom-quartile orders average roughly $0.095 in freight per unit, while top-quartile orders average roughly $0.049 per unit — nearly a 2× difference. Smaller, more frequent orders may be worth consolidating or renegotiating on freight terms.

Model note

The invoice risk flag is produced by a classifier trained on a self-defined rule, not a historical, audited outcome. Its high accuracy partly reflects the model learning that rule rather than predicting independently observed fraud or error. Treat the flag as a triage signal for manual review, not a validated risk verdict.
